Install AIO VPN

Install AIO VPN and connect securely before streaming or installing apps.

What is a VPN?

A VPN (Virtual Private Network) creates a secure, private connection between your device and the internet.

It helps protect your privacy, keeps your connection more secure on public Wi‑Fi, and can improve reliability when streaming.

Follow the guide below to install and set up AIO VPN.

Before You Start

Make sure:

  • You have a stable internet connection.
  • You know your AIO VPN login details (if you were provided any).
  • Your device has enough free storage to install an app.
1

Step 1

On your device, open the App Store (iPhone/iPad) or Google Play Store (Android).

2

Step 2

Search for AIO VPN.

3

Step 3

Tap Install (or Get on iPhone/iPad) and wait for the download to finish.

4

Step 4

Open AIO VPN.

5

Step 5

When prompted, allow the VPN permission request.
(Your device may ask you to confirm that you trust this VPN configuration.)

6

Step 6

Sign in using the username and password provided to you.

7

Step 7

Tap Connect.
You should see a status that says the VPN is connected.

8

Step 8

Leave AIO VPN running in the background while you stream or install apps.

Common Issues

  • VPN won’t connect → Toggle airplane mode on/off, then try again.
  • Login not working → Double-check your username and password for typos.
  • Streaming feels slower → Try a different server/location if the app offers it, or disconnect/reconnect once.

If you’re still stuck, restart your device and try the steps again.
