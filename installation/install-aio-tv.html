Install AIO TV

Install AIO TV and sign in to start watching on your Firestick or Android TV.

What is AIO TV?

AIO TV is our standard streaming application designed for Firestick and Android TV devices.

It features a traditional dashboard-style layout with clearly structured sections for Live TV, Movies, and Series, making it simple and easy to navigate.

This version is ideal for users who prefer a straightforward and familiar menu-based interface.

Follow the guide below to install and log in.

AIO TV Screenshots

Before You Start

Make sure:

  • Developer Options are enabled.
  • Install unknown apps is turned on.
  • The Downloader app is installed.

If you haven’t done this yet, follow the Firestick Setup guide first.

1

Step 1

Navigate to Applications in the navigation bar, then open Downloader.

2

Step 2

Enter the following Downloader code: 4078717, then click Go.

3

Step 3

The page will start redirecting.
If you see a privacy prompt, click Accept.

4

Step 4

Once the redirects finish, the app will begin downloading.

5

Step 5

Click Install, then wait for the process to complete.
Do not exit this screen.

6

Step 6

Click Open.

7

Step 7

When the app opens, you’ll be prompted to select the device you’re using.
Select your device, then click Save.

8

Step 8

You will be taken to the main login screen.

Log in

Enter the username and password provided to you.

Download the extras: Step 1

You will need to manually download each item.

Download the extras: Step 2

Items will download one at a time.

Download the extras: Step 3

Once everything has finished downloading, you’re ready to use the app.

Common Issues

  • App says “App Not Installed” → Clear some storage space, then try again.
  • Download is stuck → Restart your Firestick.
  • Login not working → Double-check your username and password for typos.

If you’re still stuck, unplug your Firestick from the TV and power source for 30 seconds before plugging back in, then try the install again.
