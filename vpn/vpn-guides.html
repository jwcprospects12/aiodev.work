What is a VPN?

A VPN (Virtual Private Network) routes your internet traffic through an encrypted tunnel to a server operated by the VPN provider. This hides your real IP address and location from the sites and services you connect to, and prevents anyone monitoring the network, such as your ISP or someone on the same public Wi‑Fi, from seeing what you're doing online.

Why use a VPN?

Streaming devices like Firesticks are often used on shared or public networks, and many streaming apps route through third-party sources. A VPN adds a layer of privacy and security around that traffic, and can help maintain a stable connection when an ISP slows down streaming traffic specifically.

Benefits of AIO VPN
